What am I looking at?

These are real neuron coordinates from the Janelia Male CNS v1.0 dataset: the nervous system of an adult male fruit fly. This exhibit samples points from 4,096 imported brain neurons out of 140,486 eligible neurons—about 2.916%. It is a lightweight view, not a complete brain reconstruction. The colours are illustrative neuron-class colours; the glow is artistic animation, not recorded brain activity.

How does curation work?

Connect your browser wallet, choose a nickname, and compare two animated artworks. Pick the one you prefer. There is no correct answer, and no Skip. Each saved choice earns one point and contributes one equally weighted preference. The leaderboard becomes available after your first comparison.

Why connect a wallet?

Your address keeps your participation count together. The site only requests account access: no signing, purchases, token approvals or transactions. The popup comes from an installed browser wallet. On mobile, open the site inside your wallet’s browser. There is no QR connection flow.

This connection-only design does not prove ownership cryptographically. Addresses are self-asserted, and nicknames are public display names, not unique verified identities.

Will my choices earn a reward?

Participation helps shape the work. Points record contribution; they are not a promise of a reward, mint allocation or financial return. Multiple wallets are allowed, and extra points do not give a vote extra weight.

What happens to my data?

The service stores your address, public nickname and total points, plus aggregate artwork preferences. Temporary retry receipts expire after 15 minutes to prevent an interrupted request from earning duplicate points. It does not keep a permanent wallet-linked list of your choices.

Are the horns and other mutations real anatomy?

No. Curation artworks may add explicitly labelled synthetic neural geometry or alter the presentation of the source brain. Original source data and creative additions remain distinguishable in each artwork’s provenance. This FAQ exhibit has no synthetic additions.
